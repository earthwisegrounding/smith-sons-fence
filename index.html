Read our reviews and you'll see the same things come up again and again: Josh shows up when he says he will, gives straight advice, prices fairly, and doesn't call a job done until it's right.

That's the whole approach. Here's what to expect from first call to final walkthrough.

  1. Free estimate

    We come out, walk the fence line with you, talk through materials and gates, and give you a clear price.

  2. Posts go in

    We mark the line and set the posts first, plumb and evenly spaced, so the finished fence runs straight.

  3. Fence and gates

    Boards, panels, or pickets go up, and gates are hung and adjusted to swing and latch cleanly.

  4. Walkthrough

    We walk the finished fence with you, fix anything you're not happy with, and clean up before we leave.
